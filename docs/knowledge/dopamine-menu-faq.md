# Dopamine Menu — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q: What is Dopamine Menu?
A: Dopamine Menu is a wellness companion app that gives you personalized menus of exercises and movements you can do based on how you're feeling. Whether you're anxious, sluggish, overwhelmed, or just need a reset, Dopamine Menu suggests quick, science-backed activities to help you feel better.

## Q: How does personalization work?
A: When you open the app, you log how you're feeling. Dopamine Menu uses your input to curate a menu of exercises tailored to your current emotional state and energy level. The more you use it, the better it learns what works for you.

## Q: Do I need any equipment?
A: No. Every exercise is designed to be done with just your body — no gym, no equipment, no special space needed. Most take between 2 and 10 minutes.

## Q: How long do the exercises take?
A: Most exercises are between 2 and 10 minutes. You can always choose a shorter or longer option based on how much time you have.

## Q: Can I save my favorite exercises?
A: Yes. Tap the heart icon on any exercise to save it to your favorites. You can build your own go-to menu from your saved exercises.

## Q: Why did I get this specific menu?
A: Your menu is generated based on the feeling you logged. Each exercise targets a specific emotional or physical state. For example, if you said you feel anxious, you'll get calming exercises like box breathing and progressive muscle relaxation.

## Q: Is Dopamine Menu a replacement for therapy or medical care?
A: No. Dopamine Menu is a wellness companion, not a medical or mental health service. If you're experiencing a crisis or persistent mental health challenges, please reach out to a licensed professional or contact a crisis hotline. In the US, you can call or text 988.

## Q: What's the difference between free and premium?
A: The free version gives you access to a limited set of exercises and daily menus. Premium unlocks the full exercise library, unlimited personalized menus, saved favorites, and progress tracking.

## Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can cancel anytime from Settings > Subscription > Cancel. You'll keep access to premium features until the end of your current billing period. If you subscribed through the App Store, you can also manage your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

## Q: How do I change my preferences?
A: Go to Settings > Preferences to update what types of exercises you like, how much time you typically have, and which feelings you want to focus on.

## Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes. Your feeling logs and activity history are stored securely and used only to personalize your experience. We don't sell your data. You can delete your data at any time from Settings > Privacy > Delete My Data.

## Q: Can I use Dopamine Menu offline?
A: Some exercises are available offline once you've loaded them. Personalized menus and new content require an internet connection.
