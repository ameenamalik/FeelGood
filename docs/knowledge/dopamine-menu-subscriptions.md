# Dopamine Menu — Subscription & Pricing Info

## Free Tier
- Access to a limited set of exercises (5–7 starter activities)
- One personalized menu per day
- Basic feeling log

## Premium Tier
- Full exercise library (all activities unlocked)
- Unlimited personalized menus — generate as many as you want
- Save unlimited favorites and build custom menus
- Progress tracking and streaks
- Priority access to new exercises and features

## Pricing
- Monthly: $9.99/month
- Annual: $59.99/year (save 50% vs. monthly)
- 7-day free trial available for annual plan

## Billing
- Subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store via RevenueCat
- You can cancel anytime — access continues until the end of your billing period
- To cancel: Settings > Subscription > Cancel, or manage via your Apple ID subscription settings
- Refunds are handled by Apple per their refund policy

## Common Billing Questions
- "I was charged but don't want premium" → Direct them to Apple's refund request process at reportaproblem.apple.com
- "I canceled but still have access" → That's expected — access continues until the billing period ends
- "How do I know what plan I'm on?" → Settings > Subscription shows current plan and renewal date
