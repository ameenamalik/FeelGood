## Description [4000]
FeelGood answers one question: what should I do today?

Open it and you get today's menu. Not a library, not a feed, not a dashboard. A short list of small things that fit the day you are actually having, each with a plain-language reason it's there.

HOW IT WORKS
- Answer a quick check-in: how you want to feel, what kind of that, and how long you have (or "Resting today"). Skip it whenever you like.
- Get a menu of three to five items on one screen: a two-minute appetizer, one main thing, a few sides that pair with what you're already doing, and a dessert that is just for joy.
- Every item says why it's on your menu, so nothing feels random.
- Don't like something? Swap it, or hide it so it never comes back.

WHAT'S ON THE MENU
Breathing resets, stretching, Pilates, qi gong, mobility, strength, walking, dancing, and more. Sessions run from two minutes to under an hour, and many need no equipment. Add your own routines, too.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
- The smallest version always counts. A two-minute day is a day you showed up.
- Coming back after time away gets a shorter, warmer menu, never a lecture.
- No streaks, no scores, no rings, no leaderboards, no calorie or weight tracking.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your check-ins and your profile are stored on your device. Optional work-arounds, such as a tender back or knees, are used on your phone to filter suggestions and never leave it. An account is optional and only used to keep your saved routines and subscription with you on a new phone. We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising or cross-app tracking.

FEELGOOD PRO (OPTIONAL)
Everything above works for free. Pro adds:
- A menu that learns from your history
- Quick adjustments and unlimited swaps
- Chat: describe your day in your own words and get suggestions. Chat is optional and asks your permission before any message leaves your device.
- Optional Calendar context, read on your device, to plan around your day

SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS
FeelGood Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or yearly. Any free trial is shown on the purchase screen before you subscribe, and only if you are eligible for it. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of any free trial. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

FeelGood offers general wellness and movement suggestions only. It is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting any new exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from birth or an injury, or managing a health condition.
