# Brand Brief — FeelGood build-in-public

1. **Business**: FeelGood — a "dopamine menu," but for movement instead of ADHD task-avoidance. You say how you feel and what you've got (20 min, home vs outdoors vs gym), it gives you something to do about it, so you skip the doomscroll-until-you-decide spiral. Menu spans workout-adjacent options (dance, yoga, qi gong, biking, walking, strength, stretching, skating, swimming) and broader self-care ones (journal, paint, do nails, clean room, cook, listen to music). Onboarding asks what's available to you, how often you want to move, and your energy level. Solo-built, SwiftUI, on-device PlanEngine, Cloudflare Worker backend, RevenueCat subscriptions.

2. **Origin story (best hook available)**: Dopamine menus were trending on TikTok/Pinterest and via the ADHD community (ADDitude's dopamine-menu template) — Ameena adapted the concept for movement. Personal root: her therapist once told her "you need to just have a dance party in ur room, put on your favorite songs, get the energy and impending doom out" — free, harmless, and it works. She's a software engineer who sits all day coding and built this for people like herself and anyone who wants a wellness app without the decision paralysis.

3. **Who**: Ameena, software engineering graduate from Western University, building solo (occasional "we" — worth clarifying if a co-builder is real or just voice).

4. **Customer (for this content)**: Two audiences that overlap — (a) other indie devs / build-in-public / Shipaton community / RevenueCat (who repost tagged entries), and (b) actual target users: people who sit at a desk all day, feel doomscroll-paralyzed about what to do for their body, want a wellness app without gamification guilt.

5. **Action**: Visibility and reposts through the Shipaton judging window — feeding into the RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 Influencer Award track (partner: Simone Sharice, @sundaiolivepilates). Ship deadline 2026-09-30.

6. **Recent story arc**: Started a "build in public Day 1, Day 2..." series, lost consistency once heads-down building took over. Sept 5–11 alone shipped 28 commits: reworked onboarding + check-in UI, "Little Wins" mascot badges (strawberry/pineapple/avocado/raspberry/kiwi), fixed real data-loss bugs (account deletion was wiping data before confirming success), kept reproductive-health data out of account sync, then a concentrated pre-launch push on 9/11 — dropped medical-claim copy, hid testing-only entry points, hardened the RevenueCat wrapper. **Current front-burner problem**: shipped the paywall, got comments saying the value prop isn't clear — she agrees, that's the next thing to fix. This is a live, in-progress story, not a closed one.

7. **Vibe**: Lowercase, warm, genuinely excited, `!!!` and stretched words (`todayyy`, `soo`), floral/soft emoji as punctuation. Talks about *feel* first, mechanism second. Says "we" even solo. NOT thought-leader/LinkedIn cadence. On video: no face-cam, no performative reactions — flat, confident delivery over screen recording/text overlay (lower edit burden too).

Accounts: IG @ameeenazara, TikTok @ameena.dev (primary), X @feelgoodios.

**Guardrail carried over from CLAUDE.md**: no medical/clinical claims. "The body keeps the score" and the therapist story are fine as personal narrative ("this is what helped me"), but copy should never frame the app itself as therapy, trauma treatment, or a clinical tool — keep it "this helped me feel better," not "this heals you."
