# FeelGood — Devpost submission draft

## Text description

Most wellness apps hand you more to decide. FeelGood hands you less.

It's a dopamine menu for movement — built for anyone whose brain already has 47 tabs open. Instead of a to-do list, which demands a decision, you get a menu: a few pre-approved options, already sized to what you actually have today.

Answer two quick questions — how's your energy, how much time do you actually have — and get today's menu: one main thing, a couple of small things, and always a two-minute option for the days that are a lot. Pick one, do it, tell it how that felt. Tomorrow's menu gets better because of it.

**The menu, every day:**
- **Appetizer** — a two-minute option, always there, never optional-feeling
- **Main** — one real session: Pilates, qi gong, strength, a walk, a ride
- **Sides** — five minutes that pairs with what you're already doing
- **Dessert** — purely for the joy of it, and it counts just as much
- **Specials** — the ones worth planning for, surfaced ahead of time

No streaks. No rings. No completion percentages. No guilt for the days you missed — come back after two weeks and FeelGood just gives you something small, not a broken streak to feel bad about.

Every recommendation says why, in plain language. The engine that builds your menu runs on your phone — nothing about your body, your check-ins, or your notes ever needs to leave it.

**Built with RevenueCat end to end:** the RevenueCat SDK powers FeelGood Pro, a real auto-renewing subscription (monthly and yearly, with a 7-day free trial) gating unlimited companion chat, unlimited menu adjustments, and calendar-aware planning. Entitlements are checked live via RevenueCat's `customerInfo` stream, purchases and restores go through the real StoreKit purchase flow, and the whole thing was verified end-to-end with a real sandbox purchase before submission — not just wired up and hoped for.

FeelGood is built for anyone who has downloaded four fitness apps and deleted four fitness apps — not because they were bad, but because each one asked you to become a different person with more free time than you have.

Open FeelGood and let today's menu decide for you.
